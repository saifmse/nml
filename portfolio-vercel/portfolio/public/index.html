About Me

Turning Ideas
Into Objects

I'm a 3D product and industrial designer specializing in photorealistic renders and precise modeling that help businesses visualize, validate, and market their products — before production begins.

From luxury consumer goods to complex mechanical parts, every model is built with engineering precision and cinematic attention to light, material, and form.
