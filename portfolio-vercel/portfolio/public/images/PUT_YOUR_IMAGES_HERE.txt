Drop your project images into this folder.

Naming convention used in index.html:
  project1.jpg  → Card 1 (large hero card)
  project2.jpg  → Card 2
  project3.jpg  → Card 3
  project4.jpg  → Card 4
  project5.jpg  → Card 5
  about-photo.jpg → Your photo in the About section

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP (WebP recommended — smaller file size, same quality)
Recommended size: 1200×800px minimum, 72dpi, exported from your render software.

See HOW_TO_ADD_WORK.txt in the root folder for full instructions.
